Basement development in Saskatoon means turning an unfinished basement into usable living space, and it's one of the most common renovation projects WLG Homes takes on. If you're planning to add a bedroom, bathroom, rec room, or rental suite below your main floor, here's what the process actually looks like from the first phone call to the final walkthrough.

## What Is Basement Development?

Basement development (sometimes called basement finishing) is the process of converting an unfinished, poured-concrete basement into a functional living space. It typically involves framing new walls, running electrical and sometimes plumbing, insulating, and finishing with drywall, flooring, and trim. In Saskatoon, it's one of the most cost-effective ways to add square footage to a home without a costly foundation addition.

## What Are the Steps in a Basement Development Project?

A basement development project generally follows this order:

1. **Design and permit application.** You decide on the layout (bedrooms, bathroom, egress windows) and your contractor pulls the building permit from the City of Saskatoon.
2. **Framing.** Perimeter and partition walls go up, along with door openings and soffit framing around mechanical runs. Read our full breakdown of [what basement framing involves and costs](/blog/how-much-does-basement-framing-cost-saskatoon).
3. **Electrical rough-in.** New circuits, outlets, switches, and lighting are roughed in before the walls close. This is where a licensed electrician needs to be involved, not an unlicensed handyman.
4. **Plumbing rough-in** (if you're adding a bathroom or wet bar).
5. **Insulation and vapour barrier.** Required by code on basement walls in Saskatchewan's climate.
6. **Drywall, taping, and finishing.** Flooring, trim, paint, and fixtures complete the space.
7. **Final inspection.** The city signs off before the space is considered complete.

## How Long Does Basement Development Take?

A standard 800-1,200 sq ft basement development in Saskatoon typically takes 4-8 weeks from framing to final finishes, depending on the number of rooms, whether a bathroom is included, and how many trades are involved. Projects move faster when the same contractor handles framing and electrical, since there's no waiting between separate crews.

## Do You Need a Permit for Basement Development in Saskatoon?

Yes. The City of Saskatoon requires a building permit for basement development, and an electrical permit for any new wiring. Skipping permits creates real problems later, from failed home inspections at resale to insurance claims being denied. WLG Homes handles the permit applications as part of every basement project.

## Should You Hire One Contractor or Coordinate Separate Trades?

You can hire a framer, then find an electrician, then a plumber, then a drywaller, and manage the schedule between all of them yourself. Most homeowners find that approach frustrating, since delays with one trade push back everyone after it.

WLG Homes handles framing and licensed electrical work with the same crew, which is the majority of what a basement development needs before drywall goes up. There's no gap between "framing is done" and "the electrician can start," because it's the same team on both.
