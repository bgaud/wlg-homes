Finishing a Saskatoon basement is a permitted construction project, not a cosmetic weekend. The City publishes the checklist. Use it.

## Do you need a building permit to finish a basement?

Yes. The City of Saskatoon [building permits page](https://www.saskatoon.ca/services-residents/homebuilding-renovations-improvements/getting-started/building-permits) lists **basement developments**, **secondary suites**, and fireplaces among projects that require a building permit.

The same page lists purely cosmetic work — painting, patching drywall, laying carpet, and similar minor finish repairs — as common projects that **do not** require a building permit when that is the **entire** scope. As soon as you add walls, bedrooms, plumbing, or new wiring, you are out of that cosmetic list.

## Rec room versus secondary suite

The City’s [Basement Development Project Guide (PDF)](https://www.saskatoon.ca/sites/default/files/documents/community-services/building-standards/basement_development_project_guide.pdf) applies to basement development in one-unit, two-unit, and multi-family dwellings. It states: **if you are developing the basement as an additional dwelling unit, apply for a secondary suite permit.**

That is a planning decision, not a wording trick. A family rec room and a rental suite are different applications.

## What to submit

The [renovation, addition, or new construction guide](https://www.saskatoon.ca/services-residents/homebuilding-renovations-improvements/project-guides/home-renovation-addition-or-new-construction) and the basement PDF together describe a complete basement file. Always required in the PDF:

- **Submission Details Form**
- **Floor plans** showing dimensions of the entire basement, labelled room uses, window location and size, unobstructed opening size, and window-well locations measured perpendicular from the well edge to the window

Other items the City says **may** be required:

- **Site plan** when adding or enlarging windows
- **Elevation drawing** (the PDF includes a sample)
- **Engineered drawings** for structural work outside Part 9 of the National Building Code, plus a structural commitment letter for field review
- **Energy Efficiency Compliance Form** for envelope, window, or mechanical alterations on houses built **after 1 January 2019**
- **Spray foam insulation request form** when using spray foam
- **Asbestos Removal Notification Form** for buildings constructed **before 1990** (also listed on the renovation guide)

The renovation guide also lists structural drawings, building elevations, and a site plan among complete-application items for renovations and basement development. If your file is larger than a simple rec-room layout, budget time for a designer. The City suggests hiring one for larger projects.

## Bedroom windows (from the City guide)

The basement guide states:

- Bedroom windows must have a **minimum opening of 0.35 m² (3.8 ft²)** with **no dimension less than 380 mm (15 in)**
- A **clear path of 760 mm (30 in)** is required between the building or window swing for an exit from a bedroom
- A window covered by a deck requires **760 mm (30 in)** clearance between grade and the deck

Do not treat those as “close enough.” Plan the wall layout around them.

The Submission Details Form also asks whether exterior-wall framing, insulation, and vapour barrier were completed under the original building permit, and whether you are installing or enlarging windows.

## Plumbing and electrical are separate

The renovation guide: **a plumbing system cannot be constructed, extended, altered, renewed, or repaired without a plumbing permit.** See the City’s plumbing project guide if you are adding a bathroom.

**Electrical and gas permits are administered by TSASK**, not Building Standards. [Electrical permits in Saskatoon](/blog/electrical-permits-saskatoon) explains the homeowner-versus-contractor split. Rough-in inspection happens **before drywall**.

## How to apply and what it costs

Apply through **[ePermitting](https://www.saskatoon.ca/epermitting)**. Building Standards: **306-975-2645**, [building.standards@saskatoon.ca](mailto:building.standards@saskatoon.ca).

[Building permit fees](https://www.saskatoon.ca/services-residents/homebuilding-renovations-improvements/getting-started/building-permit-fees) are published by the City, including a 2026 per-square-foot rate for basement development or alteration of any floor area and a minimum fee. Read the City page for the current schedule.
