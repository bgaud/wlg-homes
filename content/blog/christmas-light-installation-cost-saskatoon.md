Christmas light installation in Saskatoon typically costs more for larger homes with more roofline and extras like tree wrapping, and less for a straightforward single-storey install. Here's what actually determines the price, and what you get for it.

## What Determines the Cost of Christmas Light Installation?

**Roofline length and home size.** The more linear feet of eaves and roofline being lit, the more lights, clips, and labour time required. A larger two-storey home costs more to light than a small bungalow.

**Number of storeys.** Second-storey installs require ladder work at height, which takes longer and requires more care, especially on icy Saskatoon roofs in November.

**Extras like tree and shrub wrapping.** Wrapping trees and shrubs in the yard adds to the total but rounds out the look of the display.

**Install and takedown vs. install only.** Most homeowners want both the installation in November and the takedown after the season ends. Some packages price these separately.

**Access and roof complexity.** Steep roof pitches, multiple roof levels, or obstructions like large trees near the house can add time to the install.

## Typical Cost Ranges for Christmas Light Installation in Saskatoon

- **Single-storey home, roofline only:** The most affordable option, covering eaves and gutters on a standard bungalow.
- **Two-storey home, roofline only:** Costs more due to the added height and labour time for a safe install.
- **Full package with tree/shrub wrapping:** The highest tier, adding yard features to the roofline lighting for a complete display.

## Is DIY Christmas Lighting Cheaper?

Buying your own lights is cheaper upfront, but it comes with real trade-offs. Climbing a ladder on an icy Saskatoon roof in November carries genuine fall risk, consumer-grade lights often fail partway through the season, and most homeowners underestimate how long a proper install actually takes. Professional installation trades a bit of upfront cost for safety, a cleaner finished look, and lights that hold up through the whole season.
