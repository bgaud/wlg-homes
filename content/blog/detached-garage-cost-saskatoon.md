If you're planning a detached garage in Saskatoon, cost is usually the first question - and the honest answer is "it depends," but not in a vague way. Garage pricing comes down to a handful of specific factors: size, foundation, finish level, and electrical scope. Here's a straightforward breakdown.

## What Affects the Cost of a Detached Garage?

**Size.** This is the biggest driver. A single car garage costs meaningfully less than a double, and an oversized double with room for a workshop costs more again. Cost doesn't scale perfectly linearly with square footage either - larger garages get a bit more efficient per square foot once you're past the smallest sizes.

**Foundation type.** A standard concrete slab is the most common and most affordable option for a Saskatoon garage. Frost walls (a deeper foundation that goes below the frost line) cost more but are sometimes required by the city depending on your lot and garage size, or preferred if you want a fully heated, livable space above or attached.

**Finish level.** A bare-stud garage with siding and roofing on the outside but nothing done inside costs less than an insulated, drywalled, and heated garage. Most homeowners start with the exterior shell and finish the inside later, which is a reasonable way to manage cost.

**Electrical scope.** Basic lights and a couple of outlets add relatively little. A dedicated sub-panel, a 240V circuit for a welder or EV charger, or a full workshop setup with multiple circuits adds real cost - and needs to be planned before the walls close in.

**Garage door and window choices.** Standard steel garage doors are the baseline. Insulated doors, larger openings, or multiple doors all add to the price. Same with windows - a couple of standard units is standard, custom sizes or extra glazing cost more.

**Roof complexity and truss span.** Wider garages need engineered roof trusses rather than stick-framed rafters, which is an added material cost but often necessary for the span and snow load requirements in Saskatchewan.

## Typical Garage Build Costs in Saskatoon

For a full build - concrete pad, framing, siding, roofing, and basic electrical (lights and a couple of outlets) - here's the general range homeowners in Saskatoon should expect:

- **Single car (12x22 to 14x22):** Smaller footprint, simpler roof, less material - the most affordable option for a standalone garage.
- **Double car (22x22 to 24x24):** The most common size we build. More square footage and typically a wider roof span, which increases both material and labour.
- **Oversized double (24x28 to 26x30):** Room for two vehicles plus a workshop or extra storage - the largest jump in cost, mostly from the added foundation, framing, and roofing material.

## What Increases the Cost

- Frost wall foundation instead of a slab
- Insulation and drywall for a finished interior
- A sub-panel with dedicated circuits for a workshop, welder, or EV charger
- Insulated or oversized garage doors
- A second storey or loft space above the garage
- Complex rooflines or engineered trusses for a wide span

None of these are wrong choices - they just need to be part of the conversation from the start so your quote reflects what you actually want, not just the base shell.
