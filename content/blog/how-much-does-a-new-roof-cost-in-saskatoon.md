If you're getting quotes for a roof replacement in Saskatoon, you've probably noticed the numbers vary a lot. A 1,200 sq ft home might get quotes anywhere from $8,000 to $18,000 depending on who you call and what they're actually pricing. Here's a plain-language breakdown of what drives roof replacement cost in Saskatoon.

## What Affects the Cost of a Roof Replacement?

**Roof size and pitch.** Larger roofs cost more — straightforward. But pitch (steepness) also matters. Steep roofs take longer to work safely, require more staging, and result in more waste when cutting shingles. A steep 12/12 pitch adds significant labour cost over a walkable 4/12.

**Shingle type and quality.** Most residential roofs in Saskatoon use asphalt architectural shingles. Entry-level architectural shingles are durable enough for our climate and are the most affordable option. Premium designer shingles with longer manufacturer warranties cost more but can add curb appeal and resale value.

**Tear-off and decking condition.** A proper roof replacement starts with a full tear-off of existing shingles and an inspection of the decking underneath. If the OSB or plywood decking has rot or damage from a past leak, those boards need to be replaced before new shingles go on. This is where surprise costs come from — you won't know the deck condition until the old shingles are off.

**Valleys, dormers, and complexity.** Simple gable roofs are the most straightforward to replace. Roofs with multiple valleys, dormers, skylights, or chimneys require more cutting, more flashing work, and more time. Every penetration is a potential leak point that needs to be sealed correctly.

**Waste factor.** Material pricing is based on squares (100 sq ft). But roofing isn't a zero-waste job — shingles get cut around edges, valleys, and penetrations. A contractor should account for 10-15% waste on a standard roof, more on complex ones.

## What Does a Roof Replacement Include?

A complete roofing job from WLG Homes includes:

- Full tear-off of existing shingles and underlayment
- Deck inspection and board replacement where needed
- New ice-and-water shield along eaves and in valleys
- New synthetic underlayment across the full deck
- New drip edge on all perimeter edges
- New architectural shingles
- Flashing replacement or resealing at all penetrations
- Cleanup and haul-away of all materials

If a contractor's quote skips any of those items — especially ice-and-water shield or drip edge — ask why.

## What to Watch Out for When Getting Quotes

**Low quotes that skip the deck inspection.** A contractor who prices a roof without knowing the deck condition is either guessing or planning to charge you later. Deck replacement is a legitimate cost — it just shouldn't be a surprise.

**No mention of permits.** Saskatoon doesn't require a permit for a straight shingle replacement in most cases, but if structural work is involved, permits matter. Ask your contractor upfront.

**Vague warranty terms.** There are two warranties on a roof: the manufacturer's warranty on the shingles (typically 25-50 years, prorated) and the contractor's workmanship warranty. Get both in writing.

## How to Get an Accurate Quote in Saskatoon

The only way to get an accurate number is an in-person site visit. A contractor who quotes purely over the phone or by looking at satellite imagery is working with incomplete information. Your roof's deck condition, pitch, number of penetrations, and current underlayment all need to be assessed in person.
